Introducing Myself

Trinity College Dublin Hilary Term 2026 · About the author

Summary: An introduction to my language-learning background, academic path, and interests in applied linguistics.

Hello and Merhaba! My name is Gonca İnceören, I am 24 years old and I am doing my master's in Applied Linguistics at Trinity College Dublin. I completed my bachelor's degree in English Translation and Interpreting at Ege University in Izmir, Turkey. So my native language is Turkish and I learned English as a second language. During one year of my undergraduate studies, I took Spanish courses which helped me build a foundational level of proficiency and basic comprehension. Building on this foundation and further developing my Spanish skills are among my language learning goals.

1. Where I am from

I was born and raised in Turkey 🇹🇷 I grew up and lived for the 20 years of my life in a small coastal town near İstanbul called Bandırma, where my parents still reside.

2. Why I am here

The reason for attending this Mphil programme is to broaden my perspective and deepen my knowledge in the field of language, beyond translation studies, at one of the most prestigious and pioneering universities in Ireland, Trinity College Dublin.

3. What I hope to achieve

In the future, I hope to position myself within the field of linguistics by bringing together various disciplines, keeping up with current developments and contribute to the field innovatively.
